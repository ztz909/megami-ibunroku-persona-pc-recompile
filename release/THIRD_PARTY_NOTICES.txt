- psxrecomp runtime, Copyright (c) 2026 Matthew Stan - PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0
  (psxrecomp_LICENSE_PolyForm-Noncommercial.txt). Its own third-party parts, and who wrote them, are listed in
